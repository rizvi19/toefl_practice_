Set up once.
Practice on either device.

For you and your friend

  1. Open this site in a current desktop Chrome or Edge. No installation, API key or sign-in is required.
  2. Choose a full test or a practice task. Test your microphone, play the clip and confirm that you can hear yourself. Use headphones.
  3. Select Whisper Small English for the larger model, or Base English for a lighter option.
  4. Select Generate & cache all test audio. Kokoro prepares every possible prompt, including both adaptive routes. First preparation can take tens of minutes on a CPU. Keep the tab open; the exam clock has not started.
  5. Begin after the directions. Playback is automatic. Missing or blocked audio has a recovery button; no other voice replaces Kokoro.
  6. Speaking records automatically after the prompt and beep. The clip saves before the next prompt. Whisper transcribes the saved recordings after submission, with progress and elapsed time.
  7. At the top of the results, choose Copy answers to score with ChatGPT. It copies sources, questions, keys, answers, transcripts and task criteria. Attach your speaking recordings for pronunciation and delivery assessment.

Each device keeps separate browser-local answers, models, audio and recordings. There is no automatic synchronization. Download a backup to transfer an attempt. Do not clear site data. The site and uncached model files need internet access.

Timing and navigation

  • Reading: 19-minute first module, then 9 minutes. Back and Review stay within the active module.
  • Listening: 20 seconds per short response, conversation or announcement question; 30 per academic-talk question. Short audio is included in the 20 seconds; longer audio finishes before answer time. Forward only.
  • Writing: 6 minutes for sentences, 7 for Email, 10 for Discussion.
  • Speaking: repeat limits 8, 8, 10, 10, 10, 12, 12 seconds; four interviews of 45 seconds each. No preparation period or manual skipping.

The exact Reading and Listening profile above is the requested practice configuration. It is not a claim that every official ETS form uses those exact limits. Help, Hide Time and leaving the tab do not pause active clocks.

Feedback you can assess

Five new original forms have complete authored keys. Adaptive routes use a local 70% heuristic. Difficulty is editorial and has not been calibrated against ETS forms. Book Test 01 remains linear and its keys were verified against your supplied PDF.

Reading, Listening and sentence marks are calculated locally. Writing and Speaking require assessment. The export uses 2026 task criteria and asks ChatGPT to distinguish evidence from uncalibrated practice estimates. It cannot provide an official ETS score.

Speech transcripts cannot establish pronunciation, pacing, rhythm, pauses or intelligibility. Preserve your recordings. Optional transcript corrections are stored separately from the raw machine text.

Enter four assessed practice bands on the results page to calculate their rounded overall practice band.
