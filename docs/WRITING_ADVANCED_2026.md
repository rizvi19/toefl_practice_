# Writing expansion — 19 October 2026

Live: [Focused sentence practice](https://rizvi19.github.io/toefl_practice_/sentence-practice.html) · [Writing tests](https://rizvi19.github.io/toefl_practice_/writing.html)

## Content

**500 new individually authored sentence exercises**, in addition to the original 120. The expansion contains **200 medium and 300 hard** exercises across **50 grammar families**, ten exercises per family. Every exercise has a short contextual exchange, a fixed opening/ending where appropriate, 3–7 word/phrase slots, one complete canonical key, item-specific analysis, a grammar rule and a common-trap explanation. Selected questions include an unused tile.

The medium/hard labels are relative editorial teaching labels, not official TOEFL difficulty ratings. Some formal structures are deliberately advanced extension practice. This bank covers a broad curriculum rather than claiming to exhaust every possible English construction. It was informed by the user’s pasted Chapter 4: contextual appropriateness, complete grammatical clauses, 3–7 slots, occasional unused material and exact one-point scoring. The guide’s questions and example prose were not copied into the site.

The 50 families cover embedded wh/yes-no questions; subject/object and nested direct questions; subject/object/possessive/supplementary/reduced relatives; nominal relatives and content clauses; real, conditional-link, second, third, mixed and inverted conditionals; wish and preference; future time clauses; past sequencing; present/future perfect and continuous; layered passives; perfect modals; causatives; reporting passives; reported speech; verb and object-infinitive complements; perfect/passive nonfinite forms; preposition-gerund patterns; participial subject control; concession; reason/result; purpose/prevention; comparisons; parallelism; agreement; quantification; determiners; adverb scope; negative inversion; clefts; anticipatory it; comparative correlatives; ellipsis; mandatives; adjective complements; dependent prepositions; negative polarity; and layered synthesis.

**12 more Email and 12 more Academic Discussion prompts** bring both banks to **24**. New Email purposes include a negotiated extension, a repair follow-up, division of work, prerequisite clarification, responsible withdrawal, conflicting online information, careers-event access, a balanced noise complaint, handover, collecting forum priorities, equipment instructions and respectful review of a selection decision. Each has three required actions, a recipient/subject and specific planning advice. Each new discussion has a developed professor question, two distinct student positions and advice for extending the discussion rather than memorizing an answer.

## Capitalization

Every bank tile, including all 120 retained questions, is shown in lowercase. Placing any tile in sentence-initial slot one automatically uppercases its initial letter, whether or not that tile is correct. Moving it back restores lowercase. A fixed opening prevents false sentence-initial casing in later slots. Standard standalone “I” and weekday names are restored in the composed response. Capitalization does not affect the index-based answer key and does not reveal which tile belongs first.

## Focused practice and no repeats

`sentence-practice.html` is accessible from the homepage, Writing setup and preserved practice library. Select Medium + Hard, Medium, or Hard and any/all grammar families. Practice is untimed. Check an answer to lock it and reveal the complete sentence, item-specific reasoning, rule and trap. A correct order earns one point; an incorrect order earns zero. Backups include responses, checked history and encounter IDs.

The 500-question pool uses persistent item-level history shared with the new timed Writing blocks. An item counts when displayed, including a skipped question. Refreshing a current question resumes it rather than drawing another. Hidden questions in an unfinished timed block are reserved from focused practice; when a block ends early, unseen hidden questions become available again. Revisiting an existing question during a timed task is normal navigation, not a fresh draw. Completed feedback remains available in history.

Selection excludes encountered IDs. It **stops at pool exhaustion** rather than recycling. Filters report the remaining eligible count. A timed block needs ten unseen questions; finish a remainder smaller than ten in focused practice or change filters. Original questions remain an explicit separate legacy bank with repeatable manual selection, so old tests and frozen saved responses remain available. The new bank is the default. These protections are browser-local, not cross-device synchronization; do not clear browser storage if you want encounter history retained. Corrupt/unwritable encounter history does not silently reset the focused pool.

## Checks

Run `npm run test:writing`, `npm run audit:writing` and, with the subpath server running, `npm run test:writing:browser`.

The content/selection checks cover all 500 keys, IDs, 3–7 slots, explanation completeness, extra-tile rejection, unique contexts and answer strings, all 50 families, 200/300 level counts, all 500 items selected exactly once before exhaustion, topic/level filters, active-block reservations, encounter persistence and corrupt-history rejection. A documented lexical screen finds zero answer pairs above 0.70 Jaccard similarity. This is a duplicate screen, not proof of semantic uniqueness or difficulty.

Browser checks cover arbitrary first-tile casing and lowercase restoration in the old bank; full Writing workflow and timers; filters; immediate locked feedback; keyboard placement; reload; skip history; selected-pool and full-bank exhaustion; export; sharing encounters/reservations with timed practice; and responsive layouts. Existing Reading, Listening, Mountain and legacy library behavior is checked separately. Publication includes both the new practice route and its modules.

The source items, keys and explanations were also read for grammar, clause dependencies, naturalness and contextual sense; automated structural checks alone cannot certify language quality. These are original internally reviewed practice materials, with no external item-writer calibration or expert certification. [Machine-readable audit](WRITING_ADVANCED_AUDIT.json).
