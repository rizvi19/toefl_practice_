Format, sources & practice limits

This is independent practice for the regular TOEFL iBT format introduced on 21 January 2026. It is not an ETS product, an exact replica of its interface, or a calibrated exam.

Reading and Listening are two-stage adaptive sections. The second module depends on performance in the first. Actual times and item counts may vary. The published blueprint includes unscored items and notes that its pre-launch specifications could undergo minor revisions. Its base figures agree with the current ETS content page.

Reading uses Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life and Read an Academic Passage. Listening uses Listen and Choose a Response, Listen to a Conversation, Listen to an Announcement and Listen to an Academic Talk. The current Listening page contains an introductory count typo but lists all four tasks; the content table and specification document agree on four.

Reading permits Back, Next and Review within a module, but you cannot return to Module 1 after starting Module 2. Listening questions must be answered in order; you cannot return or replay audio. Help does not stop the clock. These navigation rules are confirmed by the user-supplied ETS Pocket Edition guide, toolbar guidance on page 15, Reading timing on page 84 and Listening directions on page 260. The supplied screenshots and video transcripts were also inspected; their variable question totals are not used as fixed official counts.

How this site simulates the test

  • Reading: 35 items in Module 1, then 15 in Module 2. Module 1 has 20 word gaps, 10 everyday questions and 5 academic questions. Upper Module 2 has 10 gaps and 5 academic questions; lower has 10 gaps and 5 everyday questions.
  • Listening: 32 items in Module 1, then 15 in Module 2. Upper Module 2 includes an academic talk. Lower Module 2 uses everyday listening tasks. These are training forms, not a guarantee about every ETS form.
  • Module 1 accuracy of at least 70% selects the upper route; otherwise the lower route. This threshold is our transparent training choice, not an ETS threshold. Both sections offer fixed upper/lower routes. Reading also offers five layouts from the supplied screenshot. Their totals vary. Difficulty tags use a relative editorial complexity rubric and have not been psychometrically calibrated.
  • Reading defaults to 30 minutes, split 21:00 / 9:00. Module 1 time and task counts can be configured independently. Listening defaults to 29 minutes, split 18:00 / 11:00 on the upper route or 18:00 / 7:00 on the lower route. Both sections allow custom module activity counts and Module 1 timing; Listening lower-route time scales at 7/11 of the upper budget. Unused time does not carry over.
  • Directions are untimed. Once started, the practice clock runs continuously, including narration, module-transition screens, Help, confirmations and time away from the page. This is an elapsed-time practice clock; it does not claim to reproduce every ETS clock behavior.
  • Listening uses a Play button for browser compatibility. Choices appear after audio ends. Each stimulus is heard once, its questions appear one at a time, and choosing an answer unlocks Next. Previous responses then lock. Reloading during playback permits an interrupted-audio recovery and marks the report as nonstandard practice.
  • All 200 active Listening activities have matched recordings: 27 existing recordings and 173 new Kokoro MP3s. New conversations alternate two American English synthetic voices. Optional browser/local Kokoro and browser English speech remain available; speech fallback depends on device voices. This does not reproduce the full official accent range.
  • Reading word gaps have fixed slots with keyboard and paste support. Everyday question groups stay together; academic questions appear one at a time alongside the full passage.

Local estimated 1–6 practice scores

ETS reports official section scores on a 1–6 scale in half-point increments from January 2026. This local model matches only that reporting scale. The published legacy-score comparison table is not a conversion from raw accuracy on this bank.

Every question and word gap receives one practice point. Unanswered items count as incorrect. The estimate is 1 + 5 × (correct items / total items), rounded to the nearest 0.5 and restricted to 1–6. For example, 40/50 yields 5.0 with this practice formula. Ended-early attempts and new Reading or Listening attempts that have not reached Module 2 have answer review but no estimated score. Task and skill feedback reports use the actual configured item counts.

This is not an official ETS conversion, a validated proficiency measurement, a CEFR result, or a prediction of your TOEFL score. ETS uses calibrated adaptive scoring, including item characteristics and unscored content; accuracy alone cannot reproduce it. This site shows a separate section estimate and does not invent an overall score from two sections.

Question bank and copyright

Reading uses 222 distinct activities: 100 Complete the Words, 30 Daily Life Short, 30 Daily Life Long, 30 Text Chain and 32 Academic Passage activities, with 1,400 scored items. These daily-life variants belong to one official task type. Exact and near-duplicate passages were removed under documented audit criteria. Listening has 200 distinct activities / 450 questions, 50 per task type, replacing repetitive template variants. All keys and option rationales cite their own script evidence. Reading forms avoid repeats within a form and prefer unseen material in the selected pool across attempts; once a pool is exhausted, least-used, oldest items return. Reading counts displayed activities; Listening counts only fully completed audio, once per session. Sound checks, failed/interrupted audio, hidden branches and review replay do not consume Listening material. Both sections share exposure history with the library. This browser-local history is not synchronized across devices. See the Reading release guide, Reading audit, Listening release guide and Listening audit.

The uploaded Complete the Words worksheets, Essential Exercise TOEFL 2026, TOEFL Preparation Book 2026–2027 and ETS Pocket Guide were read as format references. None of their protected passages, questions, transcripts, answer keys, illustrations or recordings were added to the published site. The uploaded PDFs, screenshots and transcripts remain private reference material and unchanged. Existing publisher references do not imply endorsement.

Writing section tests

Open Writing tests. The new Writing section contains 12 independently authored full forms: 120 Build a Sentence questions, 12 Write an Email scenarios, and 12 Academic Discussions. Each discussion includes a professor’s question and two distinct student contributions. Writing is linear; it does not use the Reading/Listening adaptive routes.

Full practice follows the order Sentence → Email → Discussion. Each task can also be practiced separately. Sentence items use 3–7 phrase slots, short exchanges and occasional unused tiles. Back, Next, flags and Review are available within the sentence task. Select a tile to fill the first empty slot; select a filled slot to return it. One point is awarded for each exact correct tile order, with no partial credit. Review shows the complete key and a grammar explanation for every item.

The requested sentence default is 5 minutes, a slightly shorter practice setting than the supplied ETS Pocket Edition guide’s approximately 6 minutes. Email defaults to 7 minutes and Academic Discussion to 10 minutes. Choose 6 + 7 + 10 for a 23-minute rehearsal. Timers can be customized to 0.5–60 minutes per task. Task clocks run while composing, copying, using Help or away from the page. Expiry saves and locks the current response; the next task’s untimed directions wait for you to press Start. This deliberately prevents an unattended browser from consuming your unwritten next task.

Email prompts provide a recipient, subject, campus scenario and three required points. Discussion prompts ask for a supported position in the context of the professor and student posts. Approximately 100 or more words is useful Discussion guidance, not an automatic grading threshold. No minimum is invented for Email. Both composers include a live word count, local saving, disabled browser spell check, and an independent Copy for ChatGPT button. A device or browser extension may still offer its own writing assistance.

Each copy packet contains only its own task’s full prompt, the response, timing and a fixed grading/advice instruction. Discussion packets include both student posts. Feedback requests use a task-level 0–5 holistic estimate with explicit uncertainty, coverage or contribution checks, language corrections, three priorities, an exercise and a revised response. The site sends no request to ChatGPT and has no automatic scoring service for these tasks. Copying does not stop the clock. A selected-text fallback is provided when clipboard access is blocked.

Writing stores the current frozen form and latest 20 completed attempts separately under toefl-writing-2026-v1. Writing does not change legacy library history or Reading/Listening saved sessions. Download an attempt to retain its full questions, responses and feedback packets, or download Writing history. Fresh selection prefers forms not yet started in this browser, then the least-started and oldest forms. Starting any mode counts as an exposure to that form. It is browser-local and does not synchronize across devices.

Only sentence accuracy is automatically graded. The site does not combine sentence points with invented written-task marks or convert them to an official 1–6 Writing score. Kokoro Listening audio

Before starting a Listening test, choose Kokoro (in this browser) or Kokoro (local helper) in Audio, then play Check sound. This choice is shared with the practice library's Settings. Both Kokoro options play current bundled recordings directly. Browser generation is needed only for a missing or changed recording; its initial model download is about 118 MB. Local generation reuses the Kokoro installation on the same PC and needs its helper running only when generating missing audio.

Bundled recordings play first and cover all current activities. If a future or changed item has no matched recording, only the activity you play generates audio. Audio completion still unlocks questions, and test audio cannot be replayed. Kokoro setup and generation time are excluded from the module countdown; playback and answering use the clock normally. Browser speech remains available as an identified fallback.
