# Last Day Prep — 17 November 2026

Open [Last Day Prep](../last-day.html) and choose one of five original full mocks. Each has 50 Reading items, 47 Listening items, 12 Writing items and 11 Speaking responses. These are new fixed forms; no exercises were copied from the supplied guide or existing site forms.

The supplied Official Guide was used to check task types and content ranges: 70–100-word Complete the Words texts, daily-life texts of 15–150 words, academic passages around 200 words, conversations of 35–100 words, announcements of 40–85 words and talks of 175–250 words with four questions. The new material covers purpose, inference, vocabulary, detail, pragmatic responses, grammar and productive communication. Reading and Listening keys and sentence constructions received a separate editorial review. Plausible sentence alternatives are accepted where needed. These are independent practice forms with an editorial mix of difficulty, not ETS-calibrated or proprietary adaptive tests.

Before starting, check the microphone and cache the mock’s 40 Kokoro recordings. All 200 clips were generated ahead of time with Kokoro-82M v1.0 Q4 and af_heart/am_michael voices. The browser verifies their script hash, file hash and size, then stores them locally. It does not load the large Kokoro synthesis model for these mocks, avoiding the reported browser allocation failure. No speech generation occurs during the exam. Internet is needed for the initial audio and Whisper downloads; recordings and transcripts stay in browser storage.

Every Listening recording and Listen and Repeat prompt has a three-second pause before playback. That pause consumes no answer or recording time. New Reading tasks and each new Listening source have an untimed “What’s next” page with instructions and context. Click Start to continue. Grouped questions share one playback; they do not replay the audio or repeat the introduction. Reading module time is preserved across these pages and reloads. Writing and Speaking have their own untimed stage directions.

Reading uses 19 + 9 minutes. Writing uses 6 minutes for ten sentence items, 7 for Email and 10 for Academic Discussion. Repeat recording limits are 8, 8, 10, 10, 10, 12 and 12 seconds; Interview responses are 45 seconds each. Listening uses the existing practice budgets: 20 seconds for short-response, conversation and announcement questions; 30 seconds for talk questions. Brief-response playback is included in its answer budget; long-source playback precedes the answer clock. These fixed budgets and the requested pauses are practice controls, not a claim to reproduce every operational ETS timer.

Sentence tiles move by dragging/sliding only. Clicking or tapping a tile or blank does not move it. Drag to a blank, onto another placed tile to swap, or to the bank to remove. Keyboard access provides the equivalent: Space/Enter picks up a tile, Left/Right chooses a slot, Enter drops, Backspace returns it to the bank and Escape cancels.

Speaking recording begins automatically after the prompt and beep. After submission, local Whisper transcribes all saved Speaking responses, including Listen and Repeat. Whisper Base is the default here to reduce memory pressure. Keep the raw recording: text alone cannot establish pronunciation, intelligibility or fluency. Review and correct machine transcription errors before using the result for feedback.

The result page offers **Copy answers to score with ChatGPT**, full text download and backup with recordings. The packet includes every selected question, sources, objective keys, your responses, transcripts and rubric-based scoring instructions. ChatGPT’s estimates are practice feedback. Enter judged section bands manually to calculate the practice overall score; objective raw marks alone are not an official score conversion.

Build with `npm run build:last-day`; verify all 200 audio hashes with `npm run audit:last-day`; run `npm run test:last-day` and `npm run test:last-day:browser`. See [the machine audit](LAST_DAY_AUDIT.json). Audio regeneration uses `tools/render-last-day-audio.py` with a local Kokoro ONNX model and voices file outside the repository. Model packages are unnecessary for learners using the published prepared audio.

The copied evaluation prompt includes separate 0–5 guide-based level summaries for Email, Discussion, Repeat and Interview, with guide page references (208, 217, 232, 240). The result page also displays these in a scoring-rubrics panel. It requests every objective mark, individual writing/speaking ratings, four 1–6 practice estimates with confidence/ranges, the officially reported mean-and-half-band rounding method, corrected responses and targeted drills. ETS links for the original rubrics and scoring scale are included. The original mock forms cannot supply ETS-equated section bands.
