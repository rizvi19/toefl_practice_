# Writing section release — 18 October 2026

Live route: https://rizvi19.github.io/toefl_practice_/writing.html

The homepage now offers Writing beside Reading and Listening. The preserved practice library also links to the new Writing section. Existing library Writing and Speaking, Reading/Listening content and history, Mountain lessons, and audio are retained.

## Original content

- **12 full forms**, each containing 10 Build a Sentence items, one Email, and one Academic Discussion.
- **120 unique sentence exchanges and keys**, with 3–7 chunk slots, mixed grammar patterns, occasional unused tiles, and individual explanations. Coverage includes direct yes/no and wh-questions; embedded and subject questions; relative clauses and possessives; real, hypothetical and past-unreal conditions; passive and perfect forms; comparisons; infinitives, gerunds and verb complements.
- **12 campus email scenarios**, each with recipient, subject, three required actions and task-specific self-review advice. Purposes include requests, invitations, recommendations, apologies, access inquiries, service feedback, advice, rescheduling, corrections, funding, thanks and resolving a purchase problem.
- **12 academic discussions**, each with a developed professor question, two distinct student positions and task-specific planning advice. Topics require no specialist knowledge.
- Questions are individually authored rather than generated by swapping names into duplicate templates. Content checks cover uniqueness, completeness and structural limits; these are editorial practice materials, not psychometrically calibrated items.

## UX, timing and scoring

Full and individual-task modes; manual form selection or browser-local fresh-form rotation. Fresh selection prefers never-started forms, then the smallest start count and oldest start. Starting any task mode counts as exposure to its form.

Sentence practice defaults to the requested **5 minutes**. The supplied 2026 ETS Pocket Edition describes **about 6 minutes**, so selecting 6 gives a 23-minute full rehearsal. Email defaults to **7 minutes** and Discussion to **10 minutes**. Each timer can be set to 0.5–60 minutes before starting. Unused time does not carry forward.

Clocks continue through Help, copy actions, confirmation dialogs, refreshes and time away. Expiry locks and saves the current response. The next task waits on an untimed directions screen until explicitly started. This practice choice avoids consuming an unread next task while the browser is unattended. Submitted and expired tasks cannot be reopened for editing. Sentence Back/Next, flags and Review work only within that task.

Every exact sentence key earns one point; incorrect, incomplete and unanswered orders earn zero. Every item has a post-attempt explanation. Only sentence accuracy is scored locally. The site does **not** calculate an automatic Email/Discussion grade, total Writing score, or raw-to-official 1–6 conversion.

Both composing tasks have live word counts, browser-local saving and disabled browser spell check. Device-level or extension writing aids remain outside the site’s control. Discussion’s approximately 100-word guidance is not a rigid scoring cutoff; Email has no invented minimum.

Each task’s independent Copy for ChatGPT button works during composing and in saved review. It copies its own complete prompt, frozen/current response, word count, allotted/used time and a fixed feedback prompt. Discussion includes the professor and both students. The fixed instructions request an uncertain holistic **0–5 task estimate**, coverage/contribution analysis, language corrections, three priorities, a short exercise and an improved response preserving the student’s meaning. The response is explicitly delimited as data. Nothing is automatically sent to ChatGPT. Blocked clipboard access opens selected text for manual copying.

## Storage and downloads

Writing uses `toefl-writing-2026-v1`, independently of existing histories. The active form is frozen into its session so later content changes do not alter a saved attempt. Latest 20 completed attempts are retained. The current draft survives refresh; another Writing tab’s changes reload the latest stored state instead of silently overwriting it. Downloads include full prompts/responses and individual feedback packets. Data are local to this browser; export before clearing storage or switching devices.
